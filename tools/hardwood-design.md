# Portfolio design

The site uses cream, brown, white, and beige. All surfaces are flat colors, without wood grain or material textures. Sans-serif headings, rounded cards, compact navigation, and restrained motion provide the visual structure. The homepage introduction is a single white panel; project pages use separate chapter panels.

## Colors

- Cream background: `#eee7dd`
- White cards: `#ffffff`
- Beige panels and accents: `#eae2d6`
- Brown text and buttons: `#392c25`
- Secondary text: `#6e6158`
- Accent: `#614838`
- Borders: `#e4ddd4`

## Archived texture experiment

The earlier generated texture is no longer used or included in the public assets. Its source PNG remains in the local `.dist` working directory. The generation details below are retained only as a record of that experiment.

- Mode: built-in image generation (`image_gen.imagegen`), new image; no reference image.
- Archived source: `.dist/walnut-grain-source.png`.
- Resolution: 1254 × 1254 pixels.
- Optimization: original PNG converted to WebP at quality 85, without creative edits.
- Current usage: none.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless hardwood surface texture for an understated premium mechanical engineering portfolio website. Create one square, flat orthographic close-up photograph of a single expansive American black walnut wood veneer surface. Beautiful long vertical fine grain, restrained natural variations, a few very subtle cathedral grain curves, no knots. Medium-dark chocolate brown, warm umber and muted caramel fibers, satin oil finish, soft completely even diffuse lighting. The grain should feel real, quiet, refined and finely detailed rather than rustic or artificial. Edge-to-edge material only, designed to tile seamlessly in both directions as a CSS background. No plank seams, no floorboards, no perspective, no objects, no border, no text, no watermarks, no specular glare, no vignette. Keep luminance even across the tile. Generate 1536x1536 or closest supported square resolution.
